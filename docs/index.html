The model is told to code-mix the question in its head and output only the answer.

Answers come from Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct running on Cloudflare Workers AI, prompted as in the paper (3-shot, temperature 0). Each answer is generated the first time a question is asked and then cached. Single answers can differ from the paper's runs, which average over 2,619 facts per language.

There is a flip point. Most wrong answers turn right at the native → code-mixed step; going on to full English adds little.

2

No visible translation needed. TinT recovers most of the gap in a single call, across Llama, Gemma and Qwen models from 1B to 14B.

3

It's the English words, not the script. Romanizing the question helps little; code-mixing helps a lot.

Results for all 18 languages, nine models and seven prompting strategies are in the paper. The benchmark and evaluation code are open.
